Cast web video to your TV.

CastAway is an iPhone app that casts HTML5 video from the sites you browse to your TV.

Open a page, press play, tap Cast to TV. Your phone becomes the remote.

It works with Chromecast and Google TV, needs no account and has no ads.

Also on Android

CastAway is on Android too, for phones on Android 8.0 or later with Google Play services. Already on the page in Chrome? Tap Share, then "Cast with CastAway", and the page opens in CastAway, ready to cast.

The Android app works the same way: open a page, press play, tap Cast to TV. See Support and Privacy for the Android details.
