Support

Stuck? Most problems come down to wifi. Start here, and if you're still stuck, email rye@spruik.co.

How to use it

  1. Open CastAway. Paste a link, search, or tap Browse and head to the page with the video.
  2. Press play on the video.
  3. When the "Video found · Cast to TV" pill appears, tap it.
  4. Check the video in the list and tap Cast to TV. Pick your TV if asked.
  5. Use your phone as the remote: play, pause, scrub, skip 10 seconds, change the volume or stop casting.

From Safari: tap Share, then "Cast with CastAway". The page opens in CastAway. If the app doesn't open by itself, the link is copied, so open CastAway and paste it. If the video needs a login, log in inside CastAway's browser, because it keeps its own logins separate from Safari.

What you need

  • An iPhone on iOS 17 or later.
  • A Chromecast, Google TV, or a TV with Chromecast built in.
  • Your iPhone and TV on the same wifi network.

Common problems

My TV doesn't show up

  • Make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same wifi. Phones sometimes hop onto a different network or a hotspot without telling you.
  • Guest networks and some office, hotel or apartment wifi keep devices apart from each other (called client isolation). Casting can't work on those. Use your main home network, or ask whoever runs the network to turn client isolation off.
  • Check CastAway is allowed to use your local network: Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network, and switch CastAway on.
  • Turn off any VPN on your phone while casting.
  • Still nothing? Restart the Chromecast (unplug it for ten seconds) and reopen CastAway.

The video won't play, or says "Can't cast"

  • Protected video (DRM). Most paid streaming services protect their video so it only plays in their own apps. CastAway can't cast these and will tell you so. Use the service's own app to cast.
  • YouTube and Vimeo. They have their own Cast button. Use their app.
  • Sites that need a login. Log in to the site inside CastAway's browser, press play, then cast. Some sites only allow playback on the device you logged in on, and those won't play on a TV.
  • "No video found on this page yet". Press play on the video first. Some players only show their video once it starts. If the mobile page hides the player, try "Desktop site" from the menu.
  • "The site's video link expired". Press play on the page again, then cast again.

The video stops when I leave the app

For some sites, keep CastAway open while you watch. The Now Playing screen keeps your phone awake so it won't lock. If the video stops, reopen CastAway and tap Resume on TV.
