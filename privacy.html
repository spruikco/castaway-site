Privacy policy

CastAway for iPhone. Last updated 23 September 2026.

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use CastAway, an app made by Spruik Co Pty Ltd, Melbourne, Australia ("Spruik", "we", "us"). The short version: we don't collect anything about you. The Google software that talks to your TV collects a little technical data for Google, and we explain that below.

No accounts

CastAway has no sign up, no login and no account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other details to use the app.

What Spruik collects

Nothing. CastAway has no servers of its own, no analytics of our own, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your browsing, what you cast or anything else about you to Spruik. We can't see what you watch, and we don't want to.

What stays on your phone

  • Recents. A list of your last 20 casts (video title, page address and when you cast it), so you can reopen them from the home screen.
  • Settings. A few app preferences, such as whether you've already dismissed the clipboard banner.
  • Browser data. Like any web browser, the built-in browser keeps cookies and site data for the websites you visit, so you stay logged in to the sites you choose to log in to.

All of this is stored only on your iPhone. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

If a web link is on your clipboard, CastAway shows an "Open link from clipboard" banner. The link is only read if you tap the banner, and it's only used to open that page.

Google Cast

CastAway uses Google's Cast software (the Google Cast SDK) to find and control your Chromecast or Google TV. That software is made by Google and collects some data for Google's own purposes, which are analytics and making casting work. Based on Google's published disclosures, this covers:

  • device identifiers
  • diagnostic and performance data (for example, whether a connection worked)
  • how you use the casting controls
  • approximate (coarse) location

Google states this data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. It goes to Google, not to us, and Google handles it under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The first time you cast, Google's software may ask you to accept Google's terms. Google may process this data outside Australia. To ask Google about or delete data its Cast software collects, use Google's privacy tools at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy, or email us and we'll point you to the right place.

Your local network

CastAway asks iOS for permission to use your local network. It uses it to find Chromecast and Google TV devices on your wifi and to send them the video you choose. The video only travels between the website, your phone and your TV. Nothing is saved, and none of it comes to us. You can turn local network access off any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network, but casting won't work without it.

Websites you visit

The sites you open in CastAway's browser are run by other people. They may collect information under their own privacy policies, just as they would in Safari. We have no control over those sites and don't receive anything from them.

Notifications

CastAway may ask to send notifications. It uses them for one thing: a reminder to keep the app open while a video is casting. These notifications are created on your phone and involve no server.

No tracking, no ads

CastAway does not track you, does not use the advertising identifier, does not show ads, and does not share or sell data to advertisers or data brokers.

Children

CastAway is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app includes an open web browser, it carries the App Store's top age rating.

The Australian Privacy Act

We follow the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). In plain English:

  • Openness (APP 1). This page is our full policy, and we keep it up to date.
  • Collection (APPs 3 to 5). We don't collect personal information through the app. If you email us, we collect only what you choose to send, and we use it only to reply and help you.
  • Use and disclosure (APP 6). We don't use or share your personal information for anything other than answering you, unless the law requires it.
  • Overseas disclosure (APP 8). We don't send your information overseas. Google's Cast software sends its own data to Google, which may be outside Australia, as described above.
  • Security (APP 11). The little we hold (support emails) is kept in a secure mailbox and deleted when no longer needed.
  • Access and correction (APPs 12 and 13). You can ask what we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. For the app itself, the answer is nothing.

If you have a privacy concern or complaint, email us first and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also mention it in the app's release notes.
